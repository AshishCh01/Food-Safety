Content: Mandatory hygiene checkpoints for a restaurant kitchen include:
1. **Personal hygiene** – annual medical examinations, required vaccinations, prohibition of staff with disease or open wounds, clean clothing, trimmed nails, no loose jewelry, strict hand‑washing, use of gloves, aprons, headgear and shoe covers.
2. **Pest control** – a documented pest‑control programme, trained personnel, use of traps and approved chemicals, and no visible signs of pests or infestations.
3. **Food handling and temperature control** – hot foods must reach ≥65 °C (non‑veg ≥70 °C) before serving; cooked food cooled from 60 °C to 21 °C within 2 h and to 5 °C within another 2 h; refrigerated foods kept ≤5 °C, frozen foods ≤‑18 °C; reheating to ≥75 °C for at least 2 min.
4. **Sanitation and waste management** – clean, well‑drained premises, functional grease traps, regular removal of food waste and refuse.
5. **Record keeping and training** – documentation of hygiene practices, pest‑control logs, temperature logs, training records for food handlers, and periodic internal or external audits.
6. **Compliance with Good Hygienic Practices (GHP) and Good Manufacturing Practices (GMP)** – adherence to the food safety management system required by the Act.

These checkpoints align with the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India’s inspection checklists and regulatory requirements for food businesses.
